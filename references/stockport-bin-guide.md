# Stockport Council Bin Types & UPRN Reference Guide

## 1. How to Find a Stockport Property UPRN

Stockport Metropolitan Borough Council assigns every residential property a Unique Property Reference Number (**UPRN**) for waste collection schedules:

1. Open [https://www.stockport.gov.uk/find-your-collection-day](https://www.stockport.gov.uk/find-your-collection-day) and select **Find your collection day**.
2. Enter the property postcode and select the street address from the dropdown list.
3. The browser redirects to a permanent property schedule URL formatted as:
   `https://myaccount.stockport.gov.uk/bin-collections/show/<UPRN>`
4. Copy the numeric code at the end of the URL (for example, `10090543805`).

## 2. Stockport Household Bin Colours & Contents

| Bin Colour | Waste Type | What Goes Inside |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Black bin** | General household waste | Non-recyclable household rubbish, nappies, pet waste, plastic film/bags, polystyrene. |
| **Blue bin** | Paper & cardboard | Newspapers, magazines, envelopes, cardboard boxes, greetings cards (no glitter), catalogues. |
| **Brown bin** | Glass, cans & plastics | Glass bottles and jars, food tins, drink cans, aerosols, plastic bottles, foil, yogurt pots, food trays. |
| **Green bin** | Food & garden waste | All cooked and uncooked food scraps, grass cuttings, hedge trimmings, leaves, small twigs, cut flowers. |

---

## 3. Collection Rules

- Bins must be placed at the edge of the property boundary by **7:00 AM** on the scheduled collection day (or the evening before).
- Multiple bins (for example, **Blue bin**, **Brown bin**, and **Green bin**) are frequently collected on the same day.
